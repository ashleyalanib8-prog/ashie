Ashleys Recipe for the best menudo

Ingredients

  • kg pork kasim, cubed
  • 1/4 kg pork liver, cubed
  • 3 pcs potatoes, cubed
  • 2 pcs carrots, cubed
  • 1 cup chickpeas / garbanzos
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 4 pcs hotdogs, sliced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp calamansi juice
  • 2 cups pork broth / water
  • 3 tbsp oil, salt & pepper, 1 tsp sugar
  • Optional: raisins, 2 bay leaves

Instructions

  1. *Marinate:* Marinate pork in soy sauce and calamansi for 30 mins.
  2. *Fry:* Fry potatoes, carrots, and hotdogs lightly. Set aside.
  3. *Sauté:* In same pan, sauté garlic and onion. Add marinated pork, stir until light brown. Add liver, cook 2 mins.
  4. *Simmer:* Pour tomato sauce and broth. Add bay leaves. Bring to boil, then lower heat and simmer for 40-50 mins until pork is tender.
  5. *Add:* Add potatoes, carrots, chickpeas, bell pepper, hotdogs. Simmer 10 mins more. Season with salt, pepper, and sugar.
  6. *Serve:* Best with mainit na kanin.
